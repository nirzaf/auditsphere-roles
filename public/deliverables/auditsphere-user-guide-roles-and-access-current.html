A concise operational guide for Firm Partners, Practice Managers, Client Finance Leads, and Administrators.

4. Role Cards

Engagement Partner Staff

Responsibilities: Final engagement oversight, risk evaluation, and opinion sign-off.

  • Can do: Dual-Key partner clearance, approve audit plans, sign audit opinion.
  • Cannot do: Cannot act as EQCR on the same engagement; cannot bypass client acceptance key.
  • Decisions owned: Final audit report release.
  • Screens: Strategy & Risk, Partner Sign-off, Financial Statements.
Engagement Quality Reviewer (EQCR) Staff

Responsibilities: Independent objective evaluation of significant judgments (M2-02).

  • Can do: Review all workpapers and significant judgments; provide concurring approval.
  • Cannot do: Cannot prepare workpapers; cannot serve on the operational engagement team.
  • Decisions owned: Formal concurring review clearance.
  • Screens: EQCR Review Console, Significant Judgments Memo.
Audit Manager Staff

Responsibilities: Operational fieldwork supervision, timeline management, and workpaper review.

  • Can do: Allocate procedures, issue PBC requests, review and approve audit sections.
  • Cannot do: Cannot sign the final audit opinion or execute partner clearance.
  • Decisions owned: Fieldwork completeness and review-note clearance.
  • Screens: Workflow, Workpaper Review, PBC Tracker.
Senior Auditor Staff

Responsibilities: Complex section testing, in-charge fieldwork execution, junior auditor guidance.

  • Can do: Prepare complex testing papers, review staff work, draft PBC lists.
  • Cannot do: Cannot perform final sign-off or close manager review points.
  • Decisions owned: Fieldwork section accuracy.
  • Screens: Workpapers, Substantive Testing, Evidence Upload.
Staff Auditor Staff

Responsibilities: Substantive vouching, PBC reconciliation, testing documentation.

  • Can do: Prepare workpapers, verify uploaded PBC files, record audit observations.
  • Cannot do: No self-review permitted; cannot approve own work.
  • Decisions owned: Accurate evidence documentation.
  • Screens: My Tasks, Working Papers, PBC List.
Firm Administrator Staff

Responsibilities: User management, roster identity binding, Microsoft Entra sync.

  • Can do: Assign roles and scopes, generate invitation links, manage directories.
  • Cannot do: Administrator access confers NO partner authority; cannot sign audit deliverables.
  • Decisions owned: Identity provisioning.
  • Screens: Users & Access, Roster Management, Directory Sync.
Practice Finance Lead Staff

Responsibilities: Commercial terms, engagement billing, advance fee verification.

  • Can do: Verify advance payment gate, issue client invoices, review WIP.
  • Cannot do: Cannot edit audit workpapers or sign deliverables.
  • Decisions owned: Commercial fee verification.
  • Screens: Firm Finance, WIP & Billing, Invoices.
Client Finance Lead Client

Responsibilities: Primary client liaison, engagement terms acceptance, deliverable receipt.

  • Can do: Execute Client Dual-Key acceptance, assign PBC items, receive final signed deliverables.
  • Cannot do: Cannot view internal firm review notes or workpapers.
  • Decisions owned: Engagement terms acceptance.
  • Screens: Client Portal, Dual-Key Approval, PBC Center, Reports.
Client Contributor Client

Responsibilities: Uploading requested accounting records and schedules.

  • Can do: Upload files against open PBC requests, reply to auditor queries.
  • Cannot do: Cannot sign engagement terms or view firm billing data.
  • Decisions owned: Accuracy of uploaded source files.
  • Screens: Document Upload Center, PBC Tracker.
Client Observer Client

Responsibilities: Governance and oversight monitoring.

  • Can do: Read-only access to published deliverable summaries and timelines.
  • Cannot do: Cannot upload files, modify records, or approve items.
  • Decisions owned: None.
  • Screens: Published Deliverables, Engagement Status.

6. Approvals and Separation of Duties

AuditSphere enforces strict server-side validation to satisfy professional independence standards:

  • Dual-Key Activation: Unlocked only upon Client Acceptance Key + Partner Clearance Key + Confirmed Advance Fee.
  • No Self-Review: Preparers cannot approve their own working papers or reconciliations.
  • Partner Authority Protection: System Administrators cannot sign audit reports or clear technical risks.

11. Changing or Removing Access

When personnel leave an organization or roll off an engagement, notify the Administrator immediately. Session tokens invalidate upon account disabling in Microsoft Entra ID. Temporary grants expire automatically on their end date.

12. FAQ & Glossary

Can an Administrator sign an audit report?
No. System authorization rules strictly prevent administrators from executing partner sign-offs.

What is Dual-Key Acceptance?
A mandatory control requiring both the client's formal acceptance and the engagement partner's risk clearance before audit fieldwork can begin.

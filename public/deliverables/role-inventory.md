## 2. Non-Assignable Backend Guard Role Codes & Staffing Levels (Documented in Appendix B)

- **7 Non-Assignable Guard Codes in `RequiredRoles` arrays:** `Reviewer`, `SeniorManager`, `EngagementLeader`, `Auditor`, `Accountant`, `CommercialManager`, `RecordsCustodian`. Not present in `RoleAdministrationService.AllowedRoles` or `RoleAssignmentService.RoleCapabilities`; cannot be granted via UI/API.
- **4 Engagement Staffing Levels (`StaffingLevels` in `ResourcePlanning.cs`):**
  - `ENGAGEMENT_PARTNER` (Rank 4 -> maps to `Partner`)
  - `AUDIT_MANAGER` (Rank 3 -> maps to `Manager`)
  - `SENIOR_AUDITOR` (Rank 2 -> maps to `Senior`)
  - `STAFF_ASSOCIATE` (Rank 1 -> maps to `Staff`)

## 3. End-to-End Access, Onboarding & Microsoft Capabilities

1. **Microsoft Entra ID Sign-In:** Users authenticate with their Microsoft work account (`OpenIdConnect` / `JwtBearer`). Session checks `AppUser.Status != "DISABLED"`, `SessionEpoch`, and `RoleGrantExpiry.RevokeExpiredForUserAsync`.
2. **Roster Identity Binding (`BindRosterIdentityAsync`):** Administrator pre-registers a person in `Users & Access` (`Bind a roster identity`) with `Email`, `DisplayName`, `UserType` (`STAFF` or `CLIENT`), `ClientId` (required for `CLIENT`), `BindingSource` (`APPROVED_ROSTER` or `VERIFIED_SIGN_IN`), `ExternalSubjectId` (optional), and `ExternalTenantId` (optional).
3. **Copyable Invitation Links (`/api/ui/administration/access/invitations/{id}`):** Generated when a role is granted; shares a sign-in URL. Link has no code-enforced TTL in `RoleAdministrationService` (invalidated if the underlying grant is revoked -> TTL marked `TO CONFIRM`).
4. **Client Portal Activation Gate (`ClientPortalService.CommercialOnboardingClearedAsync`, `ADR-0003`, `ADR-0012`):**
   - Key 1: Client commercial acceptance (`ACCEPTED`) of the exact current proposal revision.
   - Key 2: Staffed Engagement Partner unconditional risk clearance (`APPROVED` / `CLEARED`) and current Engagement Letter.
   - Advance Payment Gate: The 50% `ADVANCE` billing milestone is `PAID` (receipt recorded and allocated).
   - Partner Activation: Engagement activated by Partner (`EngagementActivations`).
   - First Sign-In Acknowledgment: Client user records acceptance of security terms (`PORTAL-SECURITY-2026-1`) in `ClientPortalFirstSignIns`.
5. **Optional Microsoft Capabilities (`TenantCapabilityService.cs`, `access.ts`):**
   - `DIRECTORY_READ` (`User.Read.All`)
   - `TENANT_USER_PROVISIONING` (`User.Create`)
   - `GUEST_INVITATION` (`User.Invite.All`)
   - `GROUP_MEMBERSHIP` (`GroupMember.ReadWrite.All`)
   - All optional write capabilities are disabled by default (`NOT_GRANTED` / `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`) until a customer tenant administrator grants Microsoft Graph application permissions and runs live verification. Local **Bind a roster identity** works without them.
